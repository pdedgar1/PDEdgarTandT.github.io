P.D. Edgar

Ph.D. Candidate · Texts & Technology · University of Central Florida

P.D. Edgar is a Ph.D. student in Texts & Technology at the University of Central Florida, where his dissertation examines how literary magazine submissions processes are changing in the age of AI. He holds an MFA in creative writing (hybrid memoir) and an MA in media studies from the University of Alabama, where he was Art & Design Editor of Black Warrior Review. He is the founding editor of re•mediate, an online literary magazine for computer-assisted creative writing, and an Editorial Assistant at College Composition and Communication. He grew up between Managua, Nicaragua, and Central Florida.
