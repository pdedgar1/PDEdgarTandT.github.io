Dissertation

How Literary Magazine Submissions Processes are Changing in the Age of AI

Ph.D. Dissertation · Texts & Technology, UCF · Digital Media Concentration · Projected May 2028

This project examines the ways AI technologies are reshaping how literary magazines receive, filter, and respond to submitted work — with attention to the rhetorics editors deploy in submission portals, the design of digital submission infrastructure, and the labor conditions of editors navigating an AI-inflected slush pile.

Archival & Media Projects

Literary Magazine AI Discourse & Submission Infrastructure

Primary dissertation research thread

Computational and rhetorical analysis of submission portal language, editorial policies, and AI-related statements across contemporary literary magazines. Includes network mapping of magazine ecosystems and archival work with submission management platforms.

Latin American Newspaper Poetry

Archival / Digital Humanities

Archaeology of poetry published in Latin American newspapers as a form of public literary circulation — examining how print culture distributed poetic work to non-specialist audiences and the political stakes of those republics of letters.

Ms. Magazine Poetics, 1973–1988

Presented: American Journalism Historians Association, 2023

How the poetry published in Ms. magazine across its first fifteen years shaped and was shaped by feminist notions of liberation, audience, and literary legitimacy.

Creative Research Projects

Docupoetics in 360° / Virtual Reality Poetics

Presented: Florida College English Association, 2025

Experiments in documentary poetics using 360-degree video as a medium for place-memory, exploring what an immersive, spatially-oriented poetics might look like.

Missionary Kid Identity — MFA Thesis Research

Thesis: "Dispatches from the Mission House: Scriptures of an Ex-MK" · University of Alabama, 2024

Hybrid memoir (poetry & nonfiction) excavating the life of a child raised in mission field contexts in Nicaragua and Central Florida — attending to questions of translation, faith, language, and diasporic identity.
